About

Why ASI Ventures

ASI Ventures is a personal collection of hobby projects - things built for friends, family, and my own curiosity. It's a place to learn by doing, using AI as a way to move faster from idea to something real.

Each project here started as a way to scratch an itch or solve a real problem for someone I know. Some grow into something more, some stay small, and all of them are a chance to learn.
